Nifty Trading Bot

Quant Glossary Cheat Sheet

📊 Part 1: Core Technical Indicators

1. EMA (Exponential Moving Average)

A moving average that gives more weight (significance) to the most recent price data, making it highly responsive to trend changes.

Bot Rule: Uses EMA 9 & EMA 21. EMA 9 crossing above EMA 21 confirms an active bullish trend; the inverse confirms a bearish trend.

2. RSI (Relative Strength Index)

A momentum oscillator that measures the speed and magnitude of price changes on a scale of 0 to 100 to identify overbought or oversold conditions.

Bot Rule: Used to confirm breakouts. The bot enters a Call trade only when RSI > 54, and a Put trade when RSI < 46 to ensure trade velocity.

3. ADX (Average Directional Index)

An indicator that quantifies trend strength on a scale of 0 to 100, completely independent of the trend's direction.

Bot Rule: Actively blocks trading when ADX < 20 (chop filter). Requires ADX ≥ 22 (Scalper) or ≥ 25 (Momentum) to verify that trending "fuel" is present.

4. ATR (Average True Range)

A measure of market volatility that calculates the average trading range of an asset over a set number of candles.

Bot Rule: Derived from 3-minute candles. Dynamically sizes targets (Entry + 4.5x ATR) and stop-losses (Entry - 1.5x ATR) to adapt to current volatility.

5. BBW (Bollinger Band Width)

A metric that measures the distance between the upper and lower Bollinger Bands to track market compression and expansion.

Bot Rule: Prevents entries when BBW is extremely narrow, ensuring the bot only enters when the bands are actively expanding outward.

📉 Part 2: Market Regimes & Volatility

6. PCR (Put-Call Ratio)

The ratio of trading volume or open interest of Put options to Call options, acting as a contrarian indicator of sentiment.

Bot Rule: Evaluated by the Decision Engine. A PCR above 1.0 alerts the bot to an active bearish bias; a PCR below 0.7 signals a bullish bias.

7. VIX (Volatility Index)

Often called the "Fear Index", it tracks the market's expectation of near-term volatility based on index option pricing.

Bot Rule: Halves target lot sizes if VIX crosses above 22 or 25 to protect capital from massive, unpredictable price swings.

8. IV Rank (Implied Volatility Rank)

A percentage comparing current Implied Volatility to its highest and lowest bounds over the past 52 weeks.

Bot Rule: If IV Rank is 100% (extremely expensive), the bot automatically selects ATM strikes to minimize time-decay drag.

🛸 Part 3: Options Specific Concepts

9. CE (Call Option / Call European)

A financial contract giving the buyer the right to purchase the index. Its value surges when Nifty rises.

Bot Rule: Purchased automatically when spot price, EMAs, and RSI confirm a bullish breakout regime.

10. PE (Put Option / Put European)

A financial contract giving the buyer the right to sell the index. Its value surges when Nifty falls.

Bot Rule: Purchased automatically when spot price, EMAs, and RSI confirm a bearish breakdown regime.

11. Strike Price & Premium

The Strike Price is the level at which the option can be exercised. The Premium is the cash price paid to buy the option.

Bot Rule: The bot automatically selects the exact strike and limits risk since premium paid is the absolute maximum capital at risk.

12. ATM & OTM Tiers

ATM (At-The-Money) options have strikes closest to current spot price. OTM (Out-of-The-Money) options have strikes further away.

Bot Rule: Uses ATM for high-probability scalping, and OTM (1-10 strikes deep) for explosive Gamma Blast and Zero-to-Hero lotto setups.

🛡️ Part 4: Risk & Capital Management

13. Sharpe Ratio & Drawdown

Sharpe measures risk-adjusted return (higher is better). Drawdown is the peak-to-trough drop in capital expressed as a percentage.

Bot Rule: Passive Scalper targets an elite backtested Sharpe of 5.02 with a very low 12.2% maximum historic drawdown.

14. Global Max Daily Loss Circuit Breaker

A safety feature that caps your daily loss at a specific threshold (e.g. 8% of capital or -₹4,000 for Small tier).

Bot Rule: Instantly active. If net P&L reaches the daily loss ceiling, the bot shuts down immediately, protecting your remaining capital.

15. Daily Attempt Shield

A constraint limiting the maximum number of new trade executions the bot can make in a single session.

Bot Rule: Capped at 3 trades max per day for SMALL capital tier. Blocks over-trading and prevents brokerage churn.

⚡ Part 5: Order Placement & Execution

16. Smart Limit (Price Walker)

An institutional order execution algorithm that places a Limit order and walks the price by up to 5 ticks to secure fills without chasing.

Bot Rule: Avoids dangerous market orders. Keeps slippage capped at a maximum of ₹0.25 on options contracts.

17. Broker-Side Stop Loss Orders

An exit order submitted directly to the broker's servers at the exchange immediately upon trade entry.

Bot Rule: Protects your account at the exchange level. Even if your internet disconnects or your power fails, your stop-loss remains active.
